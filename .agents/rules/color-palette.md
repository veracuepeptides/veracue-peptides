# Strict Color Palette Rule

When building, styling, or editing components, SVGs, or pages for Veracue, **ONLY** use this specific 7-color brand palette:

1. `#cb997e` — **Terracotta** (Primary accent, CTA hover states, conversion highlights)
2. `#eddcd2` — **Almond** (Borders, card strokes, divider lines)
3. `#fff1e6` — **Linen** (Light elevated card surfaces, badge fills, button text)
4. `#f0efeb` — **Alabaster / Pebble** (Main page background)
5. `#ddbea9` — **Warm Sand** (Intermediate warm badges, secondary chips)
6. `#a5a58d` — **Olive Green / Sage** (Header background, focus rings, lab badges)
7. `#b7b7a4` — **Stone / Muted Sage** (Subtle borders, line art, muted captions)

For deep charcoal contrast cards, pair with deep obsidian `#20221c` or `#191b16`.
Do not introduce outside blues, purples, generic saturated reds/greens, or unapproved colors.
